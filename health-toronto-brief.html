Five takeaways

Our summary, in their own words.

Building is cheap. Insight is not.

“…Things can get built overnight with significantly less capital.”

Lucas

“…If I can fire up Claude and get to the same thing, it's probably not that unique of an insight.”

Lucas

“…No matter what happens with AI, no one can take that asset from them.”

Lucas

Inside or outside, both can win.

“…You have a distribution network that is GreenShield at your fingertips.”

Sam

“Of course, you don't have the same return for yourself. You don't get to build equity in the companies that you build…”

Sam

“I would never ever risk $100 million with a 10% chance to succeed…”

Luke

Carriers move slowly. Plan for it.

“…If anyone has worked with carriers, they love to say, crawl, walk, run, you know, like crawling forever.”

Suzanne

“…Are you going to actually really win if you're only working with one payment provider?”

Suzanne

“…Hunting for a carrier deal first and foremost before building a strong fundamental business can actually hurt people as much as help them in the long run.”

Sam

More money is not better.

“…More capital does not necessarily drive to better outcomes.”

Suzanne

“…I met teams of 22 year olds with no money who are building at a way faster pace than you guys are because you're trying to raise money right now.”

Lucas

“Why do you need this? What are you trying to accomplish? What hypothesis do you have that you need this capital to prove?”

Lucas

Run the maze. Listen to users.

“…It's very hard to know at the beginning, but if you're good at running that maze, it will kind of reveal itself.”

Lucas

“…Every time we get stuck we're like, we're in the maze, it's fine.”

Suzanne

“…What we actually realized is people needed just help navigating all the administration associated with bereavement and grief as well.”

Luke

Closing advice

One line from each guest.

  • Stay long enough for adoption.

    “…All the successful healthcare entrepreneurs, I know their core skill set is to just cockroach their way through every single cycle…”

    Luke

  • It's fine to start uninformed.

    “…It's okay to be pretty uninformed at the beginning. AI will teach you very, very quickly.”

    Suzanne

  • Solve pricing early, not later.

    “…If you wait too long to figure those out, you'll either grow slower than you could otherwise or you'll build the wrong type of machine.”

    Lucas

  • Founding is one path of many.

    “…The traditional VC founder journey is not one for everyone…”

    Sam

Two bets and one warning.

  • Sleep health is booming.

    “…One definitely to watch is the sleep space. I feel like it is exploding…”

    “Pet insurance needs to get reinvented.”

    Sam

  • Trends rarely predict winners.

    “…They mapped the most popular trend each year of their batches versus the most successful companies in those batches and there was zero correlation.”

A Product Designer's Lens

From Evan Zhai, the author of this briefing

Evan Zhai

Senior Product Designer

  • Value moves toward understanding problems.

    Building is easy now. Knowing what to build isn't.

  • Deliver outcomes, not more work.

    Members want results, not homework.

  • Research changes what we build.

    A grief app became paperwork help.

  • Beginner questions reveal user confusion.

    If I'm lost, members may be too.

A small story from my work

I prototyped an internal tool with AI. It took about two weeks. Validating the logic took much longer. Domain knowledge was the real bottleneck.
